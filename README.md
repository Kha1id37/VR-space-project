# 🚀 Virtual Reality Space Project

An immersive VR experience built in Meta Horizon Worlds that simulates an astronaut's space run. Designed to give children and curious minds around the world the chance to explore outer space from home — something that became especially meaningful in the wake of Covid-19 and widespread lockdowns. The world features interactive elements, a driveable rover, a rocket with an elevator, a gravity room, and educational buildings to explore.

> Built by Khalid Maddred, Joe Saenz, and Richard Chi — EXCELerators 2025
> Mentor: Prof. Brian King, CSCI

---

## 🛠️ Technologies

- Meta Horizon Worlds
- Block Code (visual scripting)
- Meta Horizon Worlds Desktop App

---

## 🎯 Target Audience

- Children who want to experience and learn about space exploration
- Scientists, astronauts, and researchers
- Anyone curious about space or looking to explore new interests

---

## ✨ Features

Here's what you can do in the Virtual Reality Space Project:

- **Explore a Space Base:** Walk around a fully built space environment inspired by a real astronaut's pre-launch experience.
- **Drive the Rover:** Hop in a driveable Mars rover and explore the terrain around the base.
- **Rocket & Elevator:** Interact with a rocket that includes a functional elevator and ladder to climb aboard.
- **Gravity Room:** Experience a room with altered gravity mechanics for a unique space-like feel.
- **Educational Buildings:** Visit descriptive buildings around the base to learn about space and astronaut life.
- **Coin Collection:** Collect coins scattered throughout the world as you explore.

## ⚙️ The Process

We started with a brainstorming session, sketching out our ideas on a whiteboard. This gave us a shared vision of what the world should include — a rocket, a rover, teleportation spaces, a gravity room, and educational buildings spread across the environment.

From there, we built our first prototype in Meta Horizon Worlds, combining everyone's ideas into a single world. Once the prototype was together, we presented it to our TAs and professor to get feedback on what was working and what needed improvement.

After gathering that feedback, we created a likes and dislikes chart to organize the collective thoughts. Things people liked included the driveable rover, the rocket elevator, and the gravity room. Areas flagged for improvement included the rock textures, rover collision, the layout, the UI, and adding more functionality to the hangars.

With a clear revision list in hand, we set new goals: improve the textures, fix rover collision, add educational content tied to the rocket, and polish the gravity room. We then rebuilt and refined the world based on those goals until everything came together in the final version.

## ❓ How Can It Be Improved?

- **Rover Collision:** The rover passed through objects in the prototype. Fixing the collision system would make exploration feel much more grounded and realistic.
- **Improved UI:** The user interface could be cleaner and more intuitive, especially for younger audiences who are the primary target.
- **Hangar Functionality:** The hangars in the world were largely decorative. Adding interactive elements inside them would make the base feel more alive and purposeful.
- **Better Textures:** Some textures, particularly the rocks, felt low quality. Replacing AI-generated or placeholder textures with higher quality assets would significantly improve visual immersion.
- **More Educational Content:** Expanding the educational buildings and tying learning moments directly to key locations like the rocket would deepen the experience for students and curious explorers.

## 🚀 Running the Project

To experience the Virtual Reality Space Project:

1. Download and set up **Meta Horizon Worlds** on your Meta VR headset or via the desktop app.
2. Search for the world by name within Meta Horizon Worlds.
3. Enter the world and use your controllers to walk, interact, drive the rover, and explore the space base.
4. Visit the educational buildings, ride the rocket elevator, and check out the gravity room!
